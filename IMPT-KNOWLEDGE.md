## What is IMPT?

IMPT (impt.io) is a **blockchain-powered sustainable travel and retail platform** headquartered in Ireland. It connects users with eco-conscious hotel bookings, airline tickets, retail shopping, and carbon credit markets — with every transaction automatically offsetting carbon emissions.

**Tagline:** "Book Hotels Worldwide | 1 Ton of Carbon Removed Every Booking"

**Core promise:** Every booking on the platform removes at least 1 ton of carbon from the atmosphere automatically — no manual action required.

## Platform Overview

### Travel Booking
- **Hotels:** 7–10 million hotels available globally
- **Airlines:** 100+ airlines
- **Partners:** 20,000+ travel and retail partners
- **Pricing:** Averages 16% below competitor rates; up to 92% lower in some cases
- **Cashback:** Up to 45% cashback on travel bookings

### How Carbon Offsetting Works
1. User searches for a hotel or flight on IMPT
2. Platform calculates the carbon footprint of the booking in real time
3. Carbon credits are automatically acquired using IMPT tokens
4. Credits are minted as **carbon credit NFTs** on the blockchain
5. Each NFT represents 1 tonne of CO2 sequestered
6. When a user "retires" a credit, it is permanently removed from circulation
7. An equivalent amount of IMPT tokens is **burned** (deflationary mechanism)

### Blockchain & Verification
- Built on **Ethereum** (ERC-20 token)
- Smart contracts ensure transparency and prevent double-counting
- Third-party certifications: **Verified Carbon Standard (VCS)** and **Gold Standard**
- Carbon credits verified by: energy efficiency, waste management, water conservation, and carbon footprint criteria

### Retail Shopping
- Users shop with 20,000+ partnered retail brands
- A percentage of each sale margin is allocated to environmental projects
- Users earn IMPT tokens from purchases
- Tokens convertible to carbon credits

## Business Model

```
User books hotel/flight
        ↓
Platform calculates carbon footprint
        ↓
IMPT tokens used to acquire carbon credits
        ↓
Carbon credit NFT minted on blockchain
        ↓
User retires credit → tokens burned → supply decreases
        ↓
Platform earns commission → 50% shared with stakers
```

**Revenue streams:**
- Travel booking commissions
- Retail partnership margins
- Carbon credit marketplace fees
- Token staking fees
