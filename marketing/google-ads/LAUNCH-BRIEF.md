## PROFITABILITY CALCULATION (READ THIS FIRST)

This is the most important number in your campaign.

**Breakeven ROAS formula:**
```
Breakeven ROAS = 1 ÷ Your Commission Rate

If IMPT earns 15% per booking: 1 ÷ 0.15 = 667% ROAS minimum
If IMPT earns 20% per booking: 1 ÷ 0.20 = 500% ROAS minimum
```

**Example booking:**
- Hotel booking value: €220
- IMPT commission (15%): €33 net revenue
- Target CPA to be profitable: **below €33**
- Ideal target CPA: **€12–20** (leaves margin)

**Set Google Ads Target ROAS at 500%+ once Smart Bidding activates.**
Until then, use Maximize Conversions and monitor CPA manually.

| Launching PMax before Search data exists | PMax has nothing to learn from | Launch Search first, PMax week 2 |
| Not setting conversion values | Google optimises for count not value | Always pass actual booking €€ |
| Pausing in learning phase (days 1–14) | Resets the algorithm, wastes all data | Let it run even if CPA looks high |
| Bidding on "hotels" with no negatives | You'll pay for hotel jobs, hotel management courses | Add negatives from day 1 |

| No remarketing pixel from day 1 | Miss 80% of potential buyers | Install before first ad spend |
