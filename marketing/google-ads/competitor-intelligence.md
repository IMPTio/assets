## Competitive Landscape

### Who Is Actually Advertising on Eco Hotel Keywords

**The space is thinly contested on paid search — this is IMPT's window.**

**Tier 1 — Big OTAs (dominant spend, weak eco positioning):**
- **Booking.com** + **Expedia**: Massive budgets but eco is a filter, not a headline. Their copy: "Best Price Guarantee, Free Cancellation" — sustainability is secondary.
- **Airbnb**: Display + YouTube but minimal search on pure eco keywords.

**Tier 2 — Niche eco platforms (minimal paid search = opportunity):**
- **EcoHotels.com**: Primarily SEO. Minimal Google Ads spend. Messaging: certification badges, "every booking plants a tree."
- **Green Pearls**: No booking engine, no paid search. Zero competition.
- **Beyond Green**: Ultra-luxury (50 properties). Ad copy: UN SDG alignment, verified standards. Tiny audience.
- **KindTraveler**: Niche. "Book hotel, choose a charity donation." Minimal paid presence.
- **Ecobnb**: EU-focused, growing but small.

**The gap IMPT owns:** Nobody is running hard performance claims ("16% cheaper"), financial incentives ("earn rewards"), AND verified carbon impact ("1 ton removed") simultaneously. **This copy territory is completely unclaimed.**
